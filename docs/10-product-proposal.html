Product proposal · v1.0 · for business review

A business table platform that turns scattered company data into working processes

Companies already have their data in shop platforms, HR files, databases, spreadsheets, emails and documents. This product brings that data in, lets teams run their daily work on it with clear owners, approvals and AI help, and sends results back out as decisions, actions, reports and evidence. It ships with ready-made business scenarios, so a company can start using it on day one.

Connect what you already haveData flows in from existing systems, files and documents. Nothing has to be retyped and nothing has to be replaced.
Run work with clear ownershipEvery request, case and task has an owner, a deadline and a defined end. Nothing gets stuck silently.
Prove what happenedEvery change, approval and AI suggestion is recorded and can be shown to managers, customers and auditors.

2 · The three parts in detail

What goes in, how it is used, what comes out

Input
  • Data sourcesConnect a shop platform, a company database or a recurring file. The source system stays in charge of its data; this platform reads it and keeps it up to date.
  • Send-to-table addressAny system can send records to a table in its own format. The mapping to the table is set up once on screen, with AI suggestions.
  • Forms and collection roundsForms that pick records from existing tables, fill in the user's details, take photos and files, and read uploaded documents with AI. Weekly figures from every store, with reminders for missing submissions.
  • Documents and messagesUpload or forward receipts, invoices, contracts or chat messages. AI reads them and fills in the fields for a person to confirm.
  • ImportsExcel and CSV with saved templates, a preview before anything changes, and an error list to fix and retry.

Quality guard at the door: values are checked against field rules and the company's code lists. Anything that doesn't fit waits in a review queue with an owner, instead of being guessed or dropped.

Usage
  • My workOne place for each person: tasks assigned to me, approvals waiting, items near their deadline, anything that needs my attention.
  • Tables, boards and record viewsGrid, Kanban, calendar and gallery views, with a pop-up for each record that shows its full history and next steps.
  • Rules that move work alongStatus changes follow the company's rules. Work is assigned, acknowledged and escalated when late.
  • ApprovalsOne after another, all must agree, or any one may decide. Approvers come from business permissions, such as "may approve purchases up to ¥1,000,000", not from job titles.
  • AI help at each stepSuggests categories, checks requests before approval, drafts replies. A person decides.

Nothing gets lost: anything without an owner, past its deadline or failed goes to an exceptions inbox, and someone is responsible for it.

Output
  • Actions in other systemsA business step can call another system, for example issue a refund in the shop. The source system then reports back the new status.
  • Alerts and notificationsRules decide when to alert whom: in-app, browser push, email, SMS or chat apps, with escalation when nobody responds and delivery status on the record.
  • Notifications to other systemsOther systems can be told when something changes, or can collect all changes since their last visit.
  • Reports and dashboardsOfficial numbers with a clear owner, drill-down to the records behind every figure, and scheduled reports filtered for each recipient.
  • Data feeds and exportsFormatted Excel, CSV and regular feeds for accounting or analysis, with personal data protected.
  • EvidenceA tamper-evident record of every change and approval, ready for audits.

One direction out: the platform never overwrites another system's data. Changes there happen through that system's own actions, so each system stays the single owner of its data.

3 · How every scenario runs

The closed business loop

Each scenario follows the same loop. The loop is what turns a table of data into finished work.

Step 1CaptureData arrives from a source, a form or a document
Step 2CheckRules and code lists validate it
Step 3RouteAn owner is assigned and confirms
Step 4ActWork, approvals, AI help
Step 5ConfirmThe result is verified and recorded
Step 6MeasureDashboards show results and delays
Step 7ImproveRules and limits are adjusted

Any break in the loop, such as a missing owner, a missed deadline or a failed action, becomes an exception with a responsible person.

4 · Who uses it

People and what they do

Users are defined by what they do in the business, not by job title. The same person can hold several of these.

People doing the workHandle cases, submit requests and reports, complete tasks, confirm AI suggestions.
ApproversReview requests with AI pre-check findings, approve, reject or send back, delegate during leave.
ManagersFollow dashboards and exceptions, open the records behind any number, adjust deadlines and limits.
Business buildersAdapt scenarios or build new ones by describing them, from a spreadsheet, or by hand, without code.
Data owners and adminsMaintain code lists, fields and settings, connect data sources, grant business permissions, review access.
Auditors and external partnersAuditors see evidence trails. Suppliers and candidates see only their own records through forms or guest access.

7 · AI in business terms

Where AI helps

AI turns messy information into reliable records and helps people at each step. People make the decisions, and every AI contribution is labelled and can be reviewed.

AI systemsDescribe a need, upload a spreadsheet or point to a database, and get a working system to review.Example: "Build an asset register for our offices"
AI for tablesFills fields from documents and messages, sorts items into categories, translates, spots duplicates.Example: receipt photo → expense record
AI workflowsRoutes new items, checks requests before approval, drafts replies, answers questions and hands over to a person when unsure.Example: negative review → reply draft
AI dashboardsCreates a dashboard from a goal, writes a short summary of each update, flags unusual numbers as tasks.Example: return rate jump → task for owner
AI analyticsAnswers business questions from the official numbers, shows how it got the answer, asks when unclear.Example: "Which SKUs drive returns?"
8 · Adoption

From install to first finished business loop

First day

  1. Install the open-source version on your own server, or later sign up for the cloud version≈ 5 min
  2. Choose scenario packages; they arrive with sample data and dashboards≈ 2 min
  3. Connect a data source; AI suggests how fields match and shows a preview≈ 10 min
  4. Invite colleagues and give them business permissions from the package≈ 5 min
  5. Walk one item through the whole loop with the guided checklist≈ 10 min

Principles we commit to

  • Source systems stay in chargeData comes in from them; we never overwrite their data.
  • People decideAI suggests, checks and drafts. It never approves or rejects.
  • Works without AIEvery scenario runs fully with AI switched off.
  • Built on your operations, not job titlesPermissions describe what people do in your business.
  • Your data, your choiceOpen source, self-hosted or cloud, full export at any time.
